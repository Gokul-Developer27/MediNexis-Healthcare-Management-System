# MediNexis-Healthcare-Management-System
Online Appointment and Patient Record Management System for clinics and diagnostic centres


--> A digital healthcare management solution designed to simplify patient records, doctor appointments, medical history and clinic operations.



## About the Project

MediNexis is a healthcare management system designed for small clinics and diagnostic centers that still depend on the  manual registrations or inefficient systems to manage patient information and appointments.

My app(MediNexis) will aim to digitize and organize:

- 👤 Patient information
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor profiles
- 📅 Appointment scheduling
- 🩺 Medical records
- 💊 Diagnosis and prescriptions
- 📊 Clinic management (Admin Dashboard)
- And other features for more secure and efficient app management.



## 🎯 Problem Statement

Small clinics and diagnostic centers often depend on manual records for managing patient details, appointments and treatment history.

This can result in:

- Data loss
- Duplicate records
- Appointment conflicts
- Difficulty in accessing previous medical history
- Increased waiting time
- Inefficient record management

MediNexis solves these challenges by providing a digitalized and well-organised system for managing healthcare information.



##  Objectives

The main objectives of MediNexis are to:

- Digitize patient records
- Simplify appointment booking and management
- Maintain doctor profiles and availability
- Store patient medical history
- Manage diagnosis and prescriptions
- Reduce manual work 
- Improve clinic management
- Reduce patient waiting time
- Provide access to different users based on their role in service.



##  User Roles

### 👨‍💼 Admin

The administrator manages the overall clinic operations.

**Responsibilities:**
- Manage patients
- Manage doctors
- Manage appointments
- Monitor doctor availability
- View clinic statistics
- Manage records
--> Managing here refers to Create, Read, Update, Delete methods.


### 👨‍⚕️ Doctor

Doctors can manage appointments and patient medical information.

**Responsibilities:**
- View appointments
- View patient history
- Record symptoms
- Add diagnosis
- Add treatment notes
- Create prescriptions
- Schedule follow-up visits


### 👤 Patient

Patients can interact with the system to manage their appointments and medical information.

**Responsibilities:**
- Register/Login
- Search for doctors
- Check doctor availability
- Book appointments
- View appointments
- View medical history
- View prescriptions
